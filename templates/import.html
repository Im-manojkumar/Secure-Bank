Import transactions

Upload a CSV in one of the three formats below. SecureBank recognises the format from the columns and scores it with the model trained on that format.

Drop a CSV file here or choose one.

Every row is scored by the model, and suspicious rows open a fraud case. Rows already imported are skipped. For files over 200 MB, use flask --app app import-csv path/to/file.csv.
